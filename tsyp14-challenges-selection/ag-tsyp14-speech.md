# General Assembly: TSYP14 Speech

About 7 to 8 minutes. One block per slide. The *[italic notes]* are cues for you, not lines to read.

---

**1. Title**
Good evening everyone, and welcome to the IEEE ENSI Student Branch General Assembly. Tonight is about one thing: TSYP14, and how you can be part of it.

**2. Table of contents**
Here's the plan: what TSYP is, how we select participants, this year's theme, and the part you're waiting for, the challenges. Then we'll take your questions.

**3. TSYP**
TSYP is the Tunisian Student and Young Professional Congress. It's the biggest IEEE event in Tunisia, and one of the biggest student congresses in the world. Every year, hundreds of students from every student branch in the country meet for a few days.

**4. Video**
But the best way to explain TSYP is to show it.
*[Play the video. Afterwards:]* That's the energy we're talking about.

**5. TSYP14 Ambassador**
Your TSYP14 ambassador this year is Asma Miladi. Any question about TSYP, before or after tonight, Asma is your person.

**6. Pitching, projects, networking, workshops**
So what actually happens at TSYP? You pitch your project in front of real juries. You discover projects from all over Tunisia. You network with students, engineers and companies. And you learn in workshops. It's a few days, but people come back with friends, contacts, and sometimes internships.

**7. Awards**
And ENSI doesn't just attend, ENSI wins. 1st place at the Orange Digital Center Hackathon, 1st place in the CIS challenge, 3rd place in EMBS, and the 2023 Outstanding Large Student Branch Award. This year, we want to add to this list, and we want you on it.

**8–9. Selection criteria**
How do you get in? Three simple conditions. First, be an IEEE member with an active membership. Second, work on one of the challenges. Third, be an active member of our student branch. That's it. No CV and no grades: just commitment.

**10–11. Theme: The Noosphere**
This year's theme is *The Noosphere*. It's a big word for a simple idea: the layer of shared human thinking around the planet. All our ideas, our knowledge, our decisions, together. In a crisis, what usually fails first isn't the technology, it's how people decide together. So this year, every challenge asks the same question: how can technology help people think, trust and decide better together?

**12. TSYP14 Challenges**
Now, the challenges. For each one I'll tell you what we're building and what you'll learn. Listen for the one that fits you.

**13. AOTN**
AOTN is the startup challenge. We're building a system that warns factories when their transformer substations start failing, before it turns into a fire. Here, the business case matters as much as the tech. So if you like entrepreneurship, marketing or pitching, this is your place.

**14. NEXUS**
Flash floods are Tunisia's deadliest natural hazard, and people usually don't die from a lack of data. They die because the warning arrives too late. We're building a low-cost warning chain that still works without internet or a mobile network. You'll learn IoT, LoRaWAN, embedded systems and entrepreneurship.

**15. City Brain Convergence**
This one is unique. Three teams from three different schools each build one layer of a smart city, then plug them together live. We're building the brain: it sees incidents in the sensor data and decides what to do with an AI running locally. You'll learn agentic AI, LLMs, graph databases and IoT.

**16. IAS**
For IAS, we go into a real industry: fish hatcheries. We're building an AI and IoT system to make them smarter, safer and more productive. You'll learn embedded systems, AI, computer vision and automation.

**17. WIE**
Rural women in Tunisia grow and make great products, but intermediaries take most of the money. We're building a platform that connects them directly to customers, with fair prices, traceability, and access even without a smartphone. You'll learn web and mobile development, UI/UX and entrepreneurship.

**18. Ground Truth Under Attack**
Satellites and sensors feed decisions in farming, insurance and disaster response. What if someone fakes that data? We're building an AI security system that catches false data before anyone acts on it. You'll learn cybersecurity, AI and web development. And remember, ENSI already won first place in a CIS challenge.

**19. EnergyMind**
As AI starts making decisions on the power grid, one question matters: can we trust it? We're building an AI that detects anomalies in the grid, explains why in plain language, and knows when to hand the decision to a human. You'll learn AI, ML and UI/UX.

**20. CAS**
And maybe the most human project: a small device that translates sign language into text and voice in real time, running entirely on the device. You'll learn computer vision, edge AI and embedded systems.

**21. Booth Project**
The booth project is your free space. Robotics, IoT, a game, whatever you want. If you have an idea, we want to see it at our booth.

**22. Booth Decoration**
And not technical? You still have a place. Booth decoration is open to everyone. Help us make the ENSI booth the one everybody remembers.

**23. Thank you**
TSYP is more than a competition. It's where ideas turn into projects, and students turn into a team. So find the challenge that excites you, join us, and let's make this edition one to remember. Thank you!

**24. Q&A**
Now, any questions?

## Tips

- **Don't read it.** Learn the first sentence of each slide and the rest will follow.
- **Keep each challenge to about 30 seconds.** The challenge slides are where people decide, so keep a steady rhythm there.
- **Pause after the awards slide and after "we want you on it."** Those are your strongest moments.
